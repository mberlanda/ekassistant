# Expense Approval Policy

## Submission window

Expense reports must be submitted within thirty days of purchase. Reports
submitted after thirty days require a written exception from a finance
director before they can be reimbursed.

## Approval thresholds

Expenses under $100 are auto-approved on submission. Expenses between
$100 and $1,000 require manager approval. Expenses over $1,000 require
both manager and finance director approval before reimbursement is
issued.

## Receipts

A receipt is required for any single expense over $25. Credit card
statements alone are not accepted as a substitute for an itemized
receipt.
