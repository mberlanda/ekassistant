# Remote Access VPN Policy

## Multi-factor authentication

The VPN requires multi-factor authentication for all remote access. A
password alone is never sufficient, regardless of network location.

## Session limits

VPN sessions automatically disconnect after twelve hours and must be
re-authenticated. Sessions are not renewed automatically overnight.

## Approved devices

Only company-issued laptops enrolled in device management may connect to
the VPN. Personal devices are not permitted, even with MFA configured
correctly.
